# Upcoming-Project-List
List of Projects for Future Development
  **1. **BRS / BRD generator****
         It drafts a Business Requirements Specification from a project brief and can pull in your saved stories and diagrams. You write BRS documents for a living, so this one fits your profile best.

  **2. **Backlog prioritizer****
         Add items, score them with MoSCoW, WSJF or RICE, and get a ranked list and a chart. It needs no AI, so it works offline for every visitor.

  **3. **Meeting notes to decisions and actions****
         Paste rough notes and get decisions, action items with owners and dates, and open questions, with a stakeholder RACI view. It also works in demo mode without a key.
   
  **4. **BABOK-Grounded Chatbot Assistant****
          An intelligent, specialized conversational partner tailored specifically for Business Analysts, grounded in the standard framework of the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK Guide).
          Domain Expertise: Provides instant, accurate guidance on the six core knowledge areas, tasks, and techniques defined by the IIBA.
          Artifact Critique: Reviews user stories, BRS sections, and process flows to identify gaps, ambiguities, and alignment issues.
          Scenario Simulation: Acts as a collaborative sounding board and can roleplay stakeholders to help practice and refine elicitation techniques.
